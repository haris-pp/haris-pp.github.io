AI Video Investigator

Ask your cameras what happened.

Finding one moment in hours of camera footage usually means scrubbing video by hand. AI Video Investigator records the cameras, detects every person and vehicle, and lets you search the footage in plain English.

Type “white car at Staff Parking” or “people sitting on a bench” and jump to the moment. Watch live, scrub a 24-hour timeline and cut the clip in a second. Footage, detection and search stay on the PC.

  • YOLOv8 detection on ONNX Runtime, tracked so each object is one event
  • Plain-English search, with an optional local language model (llama.cpp)
  • Visual search with CLIP: describe how it looks, results ranked by match
  • WebRTC live wall, 24-hour timeline and clip export without re-encoding
C++17 · Qt 6 · Drogon · ONNX Runtime · YOLOv8 · CLIP · llama.cpp · FFmpeg · go2rtc · SQLite
Product site ↗Watch the demo ↗

Detection

YOLOv8 runs on ONNX Runtime and tracks each person and vehicle, so one object becomes one event, not hundreds of frames.

Search

Plain-English queries become filters for type, colour and camera. CLIP adds visual search for things the filters can't describe.

Local first

Recording, detection and search all run on one PC. No footage leaves the machine, and there is no subscription.

Demo footage: MEVA dataset by Kitware Inc. and IARPA (mevadata.org), CC BY 4.0.

RecoraCam NVR

Schedule it. Play it back. Download the clip.

Small sites with a few IP cameras usually choose between expensive cloud plans and clunky vendor software. Neither lets you just schedule recording and pull out one exact clip.

RecoraCam plans the week on a 15-minute grid, lets you scrub a 24-hour timeline to find a moment, and exports the exact clip as MP4. Every frame stays on your own disk.

  • Weekly 15-minute scheduling grid for many cameras
  • Records RTSP, RTMP, HTTP, UDP and SRT without re-encoding
  • 24-hour timeline playback with 6 h and 1 h zoom
  • Exact-clip MP4 export, with GPU or CPU transcoding when needed
Python · Flask · FFmpeg · SQLite · APScheduler · video.js · go2rtc
Product site ↗Source on GitHub ↗

Scheduling

Weekly 15-minute grid for one or many cameras, with quick fills for 24/7, business hours, nights and weekends. Plans can be copied between cameras.

Reliability

A supervisor restarts failed recorders with growing delays. Every 60 seconds, a check restarts any camera that should be recording but isn't.

Storage

When the disk passes a limit (85% by default), the oldest days are deleted first across all cameras, at times you choose.
